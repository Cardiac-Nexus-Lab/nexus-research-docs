**Date:** 13 September 2026
**Status:** Completed
**Repository:** [`Cardiac-Nexus-Lab/nexus-ai-engine`](https://github.com/Cardiac-Nexus-Lab/nexus-ai-engine)
**Code:** `src/cardiac_nexus/mri_*.py`; `scripts/train_mri.py`, `evaluate_mri.py`, `diagnose_mri.py`, `explain_mri.py`

## Objective

Build the project's second modality within the limits of an 8 GB laptop:
segment the heart in cardiac MRI, turn the segmentation into clinical
measurements, and diagnose from those measurements, with explanations that are
checked rather than displayed.

## Data

ACDC (Bernard et al., IEEE TMI 2018): 150 patients, five diagnoses of 30 each
(NOR normal, MINF myocardial infarction, DCM dilated cardiomyopathy, HCM
hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, RV abnormal right ventricle), with expert masks of
the right ventricle, myocardium and left ventricle at end-diastole and
end-systole.

Only ED and ES frames were fetched, 75 MB from a public Hugging Face mirror; the
unlabelled full cine series is 93% of the dataset and unused by published ACDC
results. Every volume was verified as 192 × 192 at 1 × 1 × 10 mm with labels
exactly 0–3. Ejection fraction recomputed from the masks matched the mirror's
stated values to 1e-14. That confirms internal consistency; because the mirror is
resampled, it does not confirm fidelity to the original release.

**Split.** The 50 official test patients (10 per diagnosis) were held out and
evaluated once. The 100 training patients were split 80/20 with four per
diagnosis in validation, generated by `patient_splits()` with seed 42. A
teammate's earlier notebook used a different 70/15/15 split of the training
patients only; the two overlap, so results from them cannot be combined. A shared
split based on the official test set has been proposed to the team.

## Design decisions made by measurement

| Question | Measurement | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Crop to a smaller input for speed? | A centred 128 px crop cuts part of the heart in 207 of 300 volumes; 160 px in 41 | Keep the full 192 px |
| Model width | Base 32: 81 ms per slice, 3.5 GB GPU memory. Base 16: 29 ms, 2.3 GB | Base 16, 2.0M parameters |
| 2D, 2.5D or 3D | 3D fits in memory but gives 200 training volumes instead of about 1,600 slices, at 10:1 voxel anisotropy | 2.5D: each slice with its two neighbours as channels |

## Training

Cross-entropy plus soft Dice pooled over the batch; AdamW, weight decay 1e-4,
one-cycle schedule to 1e-3; batch 16; 120 epochs at about 45 s each. Augmentation
excluded mirror flips, which would place the right ventricle on the wrong side.
The checkpoint was written whenever validation improved.

Validation mean Dice reached 0.895 by epoch 20, peaked at **0.914 at epoch 61**,
and ended at 0.912 at epoch 120. The model had converged by the midpoint;
the second half neither helped nor overfitted noticeably.

## Diagnosis

A classifier over ten measurements (indexed LV and RV end-diastolic and
end-systolic volumes, both ejection fractions, myocardial mass index, mass-to-volume
ratio, RV/LV volume ratio, and the difference of the ejection fractions), trained
on measurements from expert masks of the 100 training patients.

Cross-validation, 5 folds repeated 10 times:

| Model | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| logistic_C0.1 | 0.908 ± 0.055 |
| logistic_C1 | 0.913 ± 0.062 |
| logistic_C10 (selected) | 0.914 ± 0.062 |
| random_forest | 0.913 ± 0.058 |

The four are within 0.006 of one another, so the selection among them is noise.

Test, 50 patients:

| Measurements from | Accuracy | 95% Wilson interval |
|---|---:|---|
| Expert masks | 46/50 = 92% | 81.2%–96.8% |
| Model masks | 45/50 = 90% | 78.6%–95.7% |

Four of five errors are MINF and DCM confused in both directions. Both appear on
cine MRI as a dilated, poorly contracting left ventricle, and are ordinarily
separated by late gadolinium enhancement, which ACDC does not contain. The
explanations make this visible: every misclassified MINF and DCM patient is
flagged for the same measurement, a markedly raised LV end-systolic volume.

### Relation to the earlier end-to-end classifier

Sahana NS's notebook trained a ResNet-18 to diagnose directly from the scans,
reaching 98.6% on 70 training patients and 7 of 15 on validation (95% Wilson
interval 25%–70%). The figures are not directly comparable: different split,
different validation patients, and a validation rather than test set. What that
experiment established is that end-to-end diagnosis memorises at this data size,
and it is the reason this experiment diagnoses from a handful of measurements
instead. Its Grad-CAM exploration is continued below for segmentation.

## Explainability

Both methods were paired with a check that could fail.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Test-time-augmentation uncertainty as a detector of pixel errors near the heart | AUROC 0.861 |
| Volume uncertainty against Dice error (100 volumes) | Spearman +0.717, p = 4.5e-17 |
| Seg-Grad-CAM, trained against randomized weights | Spearman -0.184, maximum +0.323 |
| Seg-Grad-CAM mass inside the dilated expert heart mask | 0.422, against 0.201 for a uniform map |

Uncertainty is the more useful of the two. It concentrates on structure
boundaries and identifies the patients the model segments poorly. Grad-CAM
depends on learned weights on average and places twice the chance share of its
mass on the heart, but individual maps can resemble a random network's (maximum
+0.32), and 58% of mass still falls outside the heart.

### A check that initially failed to run

The first test run reported the randomization similarity as NaN. A randomly
initialised network gave no positive evidence for any class, so all 150 of its
rectified Grad-CAM maps were identically zero, and rank correlation with a
constant map is undefined. A CPU reproduction appeared to disagree, because on
CPU its myocardium maps happened to be non-zero. Dropping undefined comparisons
would have biased the check towards whichever maps survived, so the comparison
was changed to absolute unrectified maps, which are defined for any network,
following Adebayo et al.

## Limitations

- One dataset from one centre; no external validation. M&Ms is the natural next
  test.
- One random seed.
- 2.5D segmentation without through-plane consistency.
- Resampled third-party copy of ACDC rather than the original-resolution release.
- The diagnosis interval spans about 17 points; 50 test patients cannot support a
  more precise claim.
- The diagnosis classifier was fitted on expert-mask measurements and applied to
  model-mask measurements, a mild train–test mismatch.
- Grad-CAM localisation is well above chance but far from confined to the heart.

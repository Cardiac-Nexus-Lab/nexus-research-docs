# Plan 003a: Care Prescription Content (Draft)

**Status:** draft for review. **Not to be shown to users until reviewed by the project guide or a doctor.**
**Used by:** [Plan 003, Wellness Check Module](003_wellness_check_module.md)

Each entry fills the fields of the Care Prescription slip. Content describes the common, harmless cause and says clearly when to seek help. No drug names or doses appear anywhere.

Emergency numbers (India): **108** ambulance · **112** emergency · **14416** Tele-MANAS mental health helpline.

---

## Headache

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Triggered by | Hand on forehead or temples; lowered brows; squinting |
| What's happening | Most everyday headaches are tension headaches, linked to stress, tiredness, dehydration, long screen time or poor posture |
| How serious | 🟢 Mild in most cases |
| How fast it usually gets better | Often within a few hours with rest |
| What to do now | Drink a glass of water · rest your eyes away from screens for 20 minutes · relax your neck and shoulders with gentle stretches · eat something if you have skipped a meal · rest in a quiet, dim room |
| Medicines | Pain relief is available over the counter. Ask a pharmacist which suits you, especially if you are pregnant, have other conditions or take other medicines |
| See a doctor if | Headaches keep coming back or get steadily worse · it does not improve within a day |
| 🔴 Emergency, call 108 / 112 if | Sudden, severe, "worst ever" headache · headache with fever and a stiff neck · after a head injury · with confusion, weakness, numbness, slurred speech or loss of vision |

## Cold, allergy or runny nose

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Triggered by | Fingers at the nose; wiping the nose; sneezing |
| What's happening | Usually a common cold (a viral infection) or an allergy to dust, pollen or smoke |
| How serious | 🟢 Mild in most cases |
| How fast it usually gets better | A cold usually clears in 7 to 10 days. Allergy symptoms ease once away from the trigger |
| What to do now | Rest · drink warm fluids · steam inhalation · wash hands often and cover sneezes · for allergy, move away from dust or smoke and keep windows closed on dusty days |
| Medicines | Over-the-counter remedies for cold and allergy symptoms exist. Ask a pharmacist; some cause drowsiness or are unsuitable for children |
| See a doctor if | Fever above 102°F (39°C) · symptoms last more than 10 days · ear pain or thick, coloured mucus with facial pain · symptoms keep returning in the same season |
| 🔴 Emergency, call 108 / 112 if | Difficulty breathing · swelling of lips, face or throat |

## Chest discomfort (hands over to the cardiac module)

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Triggered by | Hand pressed on the chest; confirmed by the person |
| What's happening | Chest discomfort has many causes, from muscle strain or acidity to the heart. It should never be assumed harmless |
| How serious | 🟠 See a doctor, or 🔴 emergency if any warning sign below is present |
| How fast it usually gets better | Depends on the cause. Do not wait to find out |
| What to do now | Stop and sit down · if any emergency sign is present, call 108 / 112 now · otherwise, use the Cardiac Nexus ECG check and see a doctor today |
| Medicines | Do not take anything new without medical advice |
| See a doctor if | Any chest discomfort that is new, unexplained, or brought on by effort |
| 🔴 Emergency, call 108 / 112 if | Pain or pressure lasting more than a few minutes · pain spreading to the arm, jaw, neck or back · with sweating, breathlessness, nausea or dizziness |

## Low mood or sadness

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Triggered by | Tears; downturned mouth; head lowered. Always confirmed by the person |
| What's happening | Everyone feels low sometimes. Stress, loss, loneliness or tiredness are common causes |
| How serious | 🟢 or 🟡 in most cases |
| How fast it usually gets better | A low day often passes with rest and support. Feelings that last weeks deserve help |
| What to do now | Try the breathing exercise · step outside or take a short walk · message or call someone you trust · write down one thing that went well today · take a break from social media |
| Medicines | Not applicable here. Only a doctor can advise on medicine for mood |
| See a doctor or counsellor if | Low mood lasting more than two weeks · losing interest in things you used to enjoy · problems with sleep or eating |
| 🔴 Get help now if | Thoughts of harming yourself: call **Tele-MANAS 14416** (free, 24 hours) or **112** |

## Tiredness and screen strain

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Triggered by | Frequent yawning; drooping eyelids; rubbing eyes |
| What's happening | Usually not enough sleep, long screen time, dehydration or skipped meals |
| How serious | 🟢 Mild in most cases |
| How fast it usually gets better | Usually after a good night's sleep and regular breaks |
| What to do now | Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds · drink water · take a short walk · aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep |
| Medicines | Not applicable |
| See a doctor if | Tiredness lasts more than two weeks despite rest · with weight loss, breathlessness or dizziness |
| 🔴 Emergency, call 108 / 112 if | Fainting · severe breathlessness |
